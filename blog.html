    • Extended Reality (VR/AR)

    Virtual and augmented reality technologies have evolved from their initial uses in gaming and entertainment, blurring the lines between physical and digital realms. In education, VR is creating immersive learning experiences, allowing students to explore historical sites or conduct virtual science experiments. The healthcare industry is using AR for surgical planning and medical training, while VR is proving effective in treating phobias and PTSD. In retail, AR is transforming the shopping experience, enabling customers to visualise products in their homes before purchasing.

    AI and Machine Learning

    While AI has been a buzzword for years, advancements in machine learning algorithms have catapulted this technology into new realms of possibility. Deep learning models, inspired by the human brain's neural networks, are now capable of processing vast amounts of data to identify patterns and make decisions with superhuman efficiency. In healthcare, AI is revolutionising diagnostics, drug discovery and personalised treatment plans.
